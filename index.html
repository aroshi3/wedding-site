The Celebrations

Three days, two traditions

When March 9 – 11, 2027
Where Kathmandu, Nepal

01

March 9 · Morning

Hindu Wedding

Vivah Sanskar

Rooted in Vedic tradition, the ceremony centres on the sacred flame — agni — as witness to the vows. Expect vibrant colour, Sanskrit prayers, and rituals passed down for thousands of years.

02

March 10 · Morning

Buddhist Wedding

Poruwa Ceremony

A traditional Sinhalese Poruwa ceremony, performed on a beautifully decorated wooden platform. The ritual includes Ashtaka chants, the exchange of betel leaves, the tying of pinky fingers, and the lighting of an oil lamp — each gesture a blessing for unity and prosperity.

03

March 11 · Evening

The Reception

Celebration Evening

Family from across South Asia, the UK, Canada, and beyond — great food, dancing, toasts, and the kind of night nobody wants to end.
