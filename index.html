When

Where

Kathmandu, Nepal

Day 1 March 9

Hindu Wedding

Vivah Sanskar · Morning

Rooted in Vedic tradition, the ceremony centres on the sacred flame — agni — as witness to the vows. Expect vibrant colour, marigold garlands, and ancient Sanskrit prayers as Aroshi and Nab make their promises before family and the divine.

Day 2 March 10

Poruwa Ceremony

Morning

A traditional Sinhalese Poruwa ceremony, performed on a beautifully decorated wooden platform. The ritual includes Ashtaka chants, the exchange of betel leaves, the tying of pinky fingers, and the lighting of an oil lamp — each gesture a blessing for unity and prosperity.

Day 3 March 11

Reception

Evening

Celebrate together as the sun sets over the Kathmandu valley. An evening of food, music, and dancing that brings both families together — a joyful close to three extraordinary days in Nepal.

Make a trip out of it

Kathmandu Durbar Square

Kathmandu Durbar Square

The ancient royal palace complex at the heart of the old city — a UNESCO World Heritage Site of intricately carved temples, open courtyards, and living history at every turn.

Swayambhu (Monkey Temple)

Swayambhu (Monkey Temple)

Climb 365 steps to this hilltop sanctuary overlooking the entire Kathmandu valley — sacred to both Hindus and Buddhists, and watched over by hundreds of resident monkeys.

Chandragiri Hills

Chandragiri Hills

A cable car ride from the edge of Kathmandu up to a hilltop temple and sweeping panorama of the Himalayan horizon — from Dhaulagiri in the west to Everest in the east. Worth it for the view alone.

Thamel

Thamel

Kathmandu's electric backpacker neighbourhood — labyrinthine lanes packed with pashmina shops, thangka galleries, rooftop bars, and street food at every corner. Good for an afternoon wander or a long night out.

Boudha

Boudha

One of the largest stupas in the world and the spiritual heart of Tibetan Buddhism in Nepal. Walk its circular mandala, spin the prayer wheels, and let the chanting wash over you.

Nagarkot Sunrise

Nagarkot Sunrise

Drive up to Nagarkot the evening before and wake at dawn to a panorama stretching from Everest to Dhaulagiri — the entire eastern Himalayan horizon blazing amber and pink. One of Nepal's most spectacular mornings.

Asan Bazaar

Asan Bazaar

The oldest and most atmospheric market in Kathmandu — a labyrinth of narrow lanes bursting with spices, dried fruits, metalwork, and the daily rhythm of Newari street life. Centuries old and still buzzing.

Patan

Patan

The city of fine arts. Patan's Durbar Square is ringed with exquisitely carved temples and its world-class museum holds the finest Newari bronze and stone sculpture in Nepal — all within a living medieval city.

Visit the Living Goddess

Visit the Living Goddess

Kumari — the living goddess — is a young girl chosen through ancient ritual to embody divine power. Her ornate palace in Kathmandu Durbar Square is one of Nepal's most extraordinary cultural encounters.

Pashupatinath

Pashupatinath

One of the holiest Hindu temples in the world, set along the sacred Bagmati River. Sadhus covered in ash, ghats alive with ritual, and the scent of incense — an experience unlike anywhere else on earth.

Bhaktapur

Bhaktapur

The best-preserved medieval city in Nepal — an open-air museum of brick courtyards, the 55-Window Palace, pottery workshops, and Newari culture that has continued largely unchanged for centuries.

Try Newari Food

Try Traditional Newari Food

Beaten rice, smoked buffalo, black-eyed peas, bara (lentil patties), and local aila spirit — Newari cuisine is unlike anything else in Nepal. Eaten low to the floor, often with hands, always with company.

Paragliding

Paragliding in Pokhara

Launch from Sarangkot and soar above Phewa Lake with the entire Annapurna massif spread across the horizon. One of the finest paragliding sites on earth — no experience needed, tandem flights available.

The Last Resort Bungee

The Last Resort Bungee

At 160 metres above the Bhotekoshi River gorge, this is one of the highest bungee jumps in the world. The bridge swings over a roaring river with nothing but jungle canyon below. For the truly fearless.

White Water Rafting

White Water Rafting

Nepal's rivers offer some of the best rafting in Asia — from half-day runs on the Trishuli to multi-day expeditions on the wild Karnali. Grade III–V rapids through deep Himalayan gorges.

Jungle Safari

Jungle Safari

Track Bengal tigers and one-horned rhinos on foot or by jeep through Nepal's protected jungle parks. Unlike India, Nepal allows walking safaris — the most immersive way to experience the wild.

Kayaking on Phewa Lake

Kayaking on Phewa Lake

Paddle across the still surface of Phewa Lake with the Annapurna range reflected below you. A peaceful counterpoint to Pokhara's more extreme adventures — rent a kayak or wooden rowboat by the hour.

Momo Making

Momo Making

Learn to make Nepal's beloved dumplings from scratch — folding, filling, and steaming momos with a local family. One of the most hands-on and delicious ways to connect with Nepali culture.

Elephant Sanctuary

Elephant Sanctuary

Spend time with rescued Asian elephants at an ethical sanctuary in Chitwan — bathing, feeding, and walking alongside them. No riding, just genuine time with these extraordinary animals.

Canoe with Crocodiles

Canoe with Crocodiles

Glide down the Rapti River in a traditional dugout canoe past basking gharials and mugger crocodiles. One of Chitwan's most thrilling and quietly intimate wildlife encounters.

Ghandruk

Ghandruk

A stunning Gurung village in the Annapurna foothills — stone-paved lanes, traditional homestays, and front-row views of Annapurna South and Machhapuchhre. A half-day drive and short hike from Pokhara.

Mustang

Mustang

The forbidden kingdom — a remote, arid plateau north of the Annapurnas with ancient cave cities, Tibetan-style monasteries, and a landscape unlike anywhere else in Nepal. A flight from Pokhara opens this otherworldly corner.

Art Class

Art Class

Try your hand at traditional Thangka painting or Nepali pottery with a local artist. A slow, meditative experience that gives you something genuinely handmade to bring home.

10–18 days

Annapurna Base Camp

Annapurna Base Camp

Through rhododendron forests, Gurung villages, and high alpine terrain to a natural amphitheatre at 4,130m, ringed entirely by some of the world's highest peaks. One of the great treks of Asia.

12–15 days

Everest Base Camp

Everest Base Camp

The world's most iconic trek. Fly into Lukla, walk through Sherpa villages and rhododendron valleys to 5,364m at the foot of the world's highest mountain. The Khumbu icefall visible right in front of you.

3 days

Mardi Himal · Poon Hill

Mardi Himal or Poon Hill

Nepal's best short treks. Poon Hill delivers a legendary 360° Himalayan sunrise at 3,210m. Mardi Himal takes you up a quieter ridge to dramatic close-up views of Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) and the Annapurnas.

1–2 days

Nagarkot–Dhulikhel

Nagarkot–Dhulikhel

A gentle ridge walk just outside Kathmandu — through terraced fields and pine forest with Himalayan views the whole way. Perfect for those who want mountain scenery without committing to a full expedition.

1 day

Namo Buddha

Namo Buddha

A half-day hike or short drive from Kathmandu to one of the most sacred Buddhist pilgrimage sites in the world. A hilltop monastery, ancient stupas, and sweeping valley views make it well worth the trip.
